## Question

Offline results older than 7 days are silently stamped with the time they were finally sent, and Safari can clear local storage for sites unused for about 7 days. Decide: the maximum age accepted, what the inspector sees when a queued result is old or cannot be sent, whether the true inspection time is kept in a separate column, and what is done about data that may already have been evicted. Informed by what the real-phone run shows about the queue.
