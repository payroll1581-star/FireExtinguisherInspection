## Question

The web app runs as one owner account ("Execute as me"). If that person leaves or the account is suspended, inspections stop and alerts stop. Decide: who owns the Sheet, script, Drive folders and triggers; whether a second owner or a shared organisational account is used; how ownership is transferred; and what the operator does on the day the owner is unavailable.
