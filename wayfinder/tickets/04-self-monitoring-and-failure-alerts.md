## Question

If the daily or monthly trigger fails, a request throws, or a backup fails, nobody is told. Decide what counts as a failure worth telling the operator about, how they are told (email, a status cell on a sheet, a menu "system check"), how often a "still alive" signal is sent, where errors are logged in a form a non-developer can read, and how noisy it is allowed to be.
